# Topic-to-Research Handoff Contract

Protocol: `DV-TOPIC-RESEARCH-V1`

Transfer:
- frozen Research Question;
- scope;
- target venue/band;
- field/venue maps;
- anchor papers;
- closest prior work;
- each load-bearing paper's evidence access level and source state;
- outstanding `SOURCE REQUESTS`;
- `BLOCKED_BY_SOURCE` and `SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE` items;
- gap status and search envelope;
- competitors;
- datasets/baselines/metrics only where actually verified;
- compute/equipment/cost constraints;
- fatal risks;
- rejected alternatives;
- first decisive test;
- unresolved questions.

Do not upgrade an unresolved source in the receiving research workflow.
A missing full text remains missing until the receiving workflow actually inspects it.

Transfer evidence and provenance, not a guarantee of novelty, publishability, method correctness, or experimental success.
