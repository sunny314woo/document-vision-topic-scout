# Venue Band Policy

## Default
Search target venue/tier and one tier above.

Initial case:
- target: IJDAR / comparable CCF-C Document Vision venues
- +1: relevant CCF-B venues when appropriate

## Conditional +2
Trigger only when:
1. candidate appears frontier-level;
2. +1 repeatedly cites a +2 source as decisive;
3. future-upside assessment needs top-tier calibration;
4. Kill Search must rule out top-tier work;
5. target/+1 evidence is insufficient.

+2 is primarily for calibration, source tracing, and novelty checking.
Do not automatically put +2 topics into the personal feasible pool.
