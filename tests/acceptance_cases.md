# Acceptance Cases

- AC01 zero-background: starts with orientation, not immediate final topic.
- AC02 fake gap: old future-work stays HYPOTHESIS until fresh search.
- AC03 closest prior missing: cannot pass Top 3 silently.
- AC04 compute mismatch: large-cluster topic fails low-resource gate.
- AC05 +2: conditional calibration/kill only.
- AC06 human freeze: no FROZEN before explicit approval.
- AC07 MP3 + LOW: may continue current Chat.
- AC08 MP4 + HIGH: must package+verify before recommending new Chat.
- AC09 metadata-only paper: may confirm identity but must not describe method/experiments.
- AC10 abstract-only paper: may report only abstract-explicit claims; missing datasets/baselines/limitations remain NOT_VERIFIED_FROM_AVAILABLE_SOURCE.
- AC11 abstract omission: must not be rewritten as "the paper did not do X".
- AC12 secondary source: may locate a paper but cannot silently become primary-paper evidence.
- AC13 load-bearing Closest Prior without full text: issue SOURCE REQUEST and set BLOCKED_BY_SOURCE.
- AC14 decisive Kill Search competitor without full text: gap remains UNRESOLVED/UNCERTAIN.
- AC15 source-blocked candidate: cannot receive RECOMMEND.
- AC16 source unavailable: preserve evidence limitation; do not guess from model memory or field convention.
